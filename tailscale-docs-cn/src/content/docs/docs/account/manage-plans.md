Use these resources to understand Tailscale's plans and how to modify your plan.

[**Upgrade your plan**](/docs/account/manage-plans/upgrade-plan) — Upgrade your Tailscale plan, whether you are moving from the Personal plan to a paid plan or switching between paid plans.

[**Modify an existing plan**](/docs/account/manage-plans/modify-existing-plan) — You can modify your existing plan to add seats, remove seats, and purchase features such as Mullvad exit nodes.

[**Downgrade your plan**](/docs/account/manage-plans/downgrade-plan) — Downgrade your Tailscale plan to a different plan or the free Personal plan.

[**Change your legacy plan**](/docs/account/manage-plans/legacy-plans) — Migrate or upgrade your legacy Tailscale plan to a newer plan.

[**Free pricing plans and discounts**](/docs/account/manage-plans/free-plans-discounts) — Explore the free plans and discount pricing offered by Tailscale.

[**Pricing ↗**](https://tailscale.com/pricing) — View and compare all current Tailscale plans.

[**Pricing FAQs ↗**](https://tailscale.com/pricing#faqs) — Get answers to common questions related to plans and billing.
