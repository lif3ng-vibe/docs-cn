## Bugsink 是什么？

[Bugsink](https://www.bugsink.com/?utm_source=coolify.io) 为你的应用提供[错误跟踪](https://www.bugsink.com/error-tracking/?utm_source=coolify.io)能力，并通过自托管让你拥有完全的控制权。

- [为自托管而生](https://www.bugsink.com/built-to-self-host/?utm_source=coolify.io)
- [兼容 Sentry SDK](https://www.bugsink.com/sentry-sdk-compatible/?utm_source=coolify.io)
- [可扩展且可靠](https://www.bugsink.com/scalable-and-reliable/?utm_source=coolify.io)
